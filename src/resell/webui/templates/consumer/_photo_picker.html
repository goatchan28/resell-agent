{# Picking photos, one or many, camera or library. `capture` is deliberately absent. With it, iOS Safari goes straight to the camera, takes exactly one photo, and ignores `multiple` -- which is why the first version allowed a single shot and offered no way into the library. Left off, Safari shows its own sheet: Photo Library (multi-select), Take Photo, and Choose File. That one control covers both things the seller might mean. Selections accumulate rather than replace, so "Take Photo" three times leaves three photos rather than the last one. The browser hands back a read-only FileList each time; a DataTransfer is the only way to build a new one. Where that is missing the input keeps its own last selection, which is the old behaviour and still works. Nothing submits itself. A listing has several photos and the seller decides when they have taken enough. #}
